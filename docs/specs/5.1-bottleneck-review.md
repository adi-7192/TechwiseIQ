# Spec — ROADMAP 5.1 free 20-minute bottleneck review page

Status: PM spec 2026-10-04, owner answers applied (same 20-min call as /contact step 2; no
written deliverable; [NEW] lines approved; placement approved).

## Route + metadata
- `/bottleneck-review`, static. title `Free 20-minute bottleneck review`; description: "Tell us
  what's slowing your business down. A free 20-minute call: you explain the problem, we explain
  how we'd fix it. Reply within 24 hours. Dubai-based, working worldwide."
- canonical + `socialMetadata` like /contact. JSON-LD one `Service`, provider
  `{'@id':'https://techwiseiq.com/#organization'}`, no Offer/price/priceRange (D-003).
- `ImmersiveShell scene="advisory"` + SiteHeader + SiteFooter (same as /contact).

## Sections
1. Hero — SectionLabel "Free · 20 minutes · Dubai / Worldwide"; h1 "The free 20-minute
   <accent>bottleneck review.</accent>"; lede "Something in your business is slower than it should
   be. Bring it to us. **Twenty minutes, free**: you explain the mess, we explain how we'd untangle it."
2. How it works (h2, `<ol role="list">`):
   1. **You tell us.** Fill in the form below: what's stuck and what you use today.
   2. **We reply.** A real person reads it and replies **within 24 hours** to set up the call.
   3. **The 20-minute call.** You explain the problem. We explain how we'd untangle it.
   4. **Then it's your call.** Want us to build it? We bring options with our recommendation, then
      the scope and price in writing. Not for you? **No hard feelings.**
3. Good things to bring (h2, 4 items): A website that gets visits but no enquiries. · Copying the
   same data between spreadsheets by hand. · An inbox your team sorts all day. · A tool that almost
   does what you need.
4. What it is (and isn't): "It's a conversation, **not a report and not a quote**. If you want a
   quote after, it comes in writing for the option you choose. No packages, no price list."
5. Form (h2 "Tell us what's stuck.") — existing ContactForm with `kind="review"`: hidden
   `inquiry=review`, submit "Request my free review". Line under: "Prefer email?
   Info@<wbr />techwiseiqtechnologies.ae". No sidebar / WhatsApp buttons. Success = same as /contact.

## Design
Scene `advisory`, acid only (`--tw-accent`); no proof object, no new motion (ambient scene only,
no Reveal; nothing to change for reduced motion). Rhythm: sparse hero → dense steps → dense
list → sparse statement → dense form. Imports: `c` = `contact/contact.module.css`,
`s` = `ServicesOverview/ServicesOverview.module.css`, `p` = new `bottleneck-review.module.css`.
No SectionLabels on h2s (would add copy). Copy verbatim; splitting a bold lead into an h3 is not a
wording change.

1. **Hero** — `<section className={c.hero}><div className="tw-wrap">`, `SectionLabel`, then
   `DisplayHeading as="h1" size="statement" className={p.title}` with `<em>bottleneck review.</em>`
   (the primitive turns `em` acid). Lede: `<p className={c.intro}>` with `<strong>`.
   Why `statement`, not `c.title`/hero: at 390 the hero token is 68px and "bottleneck" would run
   past the 354px column. Statement (48px at 390, ~86px at 1440) is what /services uses for its h1.
   390 and 1440: one column, h1 left-aligned, lede capped at 55ch by `c.intro`.
2. **How it works** — `Section ruled density="dense" aria-labelledby="how-title"`;
   `DisplayHeading as="h2" size="h2" id="how-title" className={s.sectionTitle}`;
   `<ol role="list" className={s.deliveryTrack}>`, the same markup as /services #engage:
   `span.deliveryMarker` "01"–"04", `h3` = bold lead ("You tell us."), `p` = the rest, with any
   inner `<strong>` kept. 1440: 4 columns with rules on top. 768: 2x2. 480 and below (so 390): 1 column.
3. **Good things to bring** — `Section ruled density="dense"`, `<div className={s.directoryIntro}>`
   (h2 left, `<ul role="list" className={s.capabilities}>` right). 1440: 1.3fr/1fr split, list
   2x2 with rules on top. ≤768: stacked. ≤480: list 1 column.
4. **What it is (and isn't)** — `Section ruled density="sparse"`, `s.directoryIntro` with h2 left
   and `<p className={s.sectionBody}>` right (`<strong>` on "not a report and not a quote").
   The only thing in this section is the h2 with its paragraph. 390: stacked.
5. **Form** — `Section ruled density="dense" id="review-form" aria-labelledby="form-title"`;
   h2 as above; `<div className={p.formCol}>` holding `<ContactForm review />`, then
   `<p className={`${c.nextNote} u-breakable`}>Prefer email? <a href="mailto:…">Info@<wbr />
   techwiseiqtechnologies.ae</a></p>` (`c.nextNote a` already gives an underlined fg link and
   44px on coarse pointers). 1440: one column capped at 46rem, left-aligned, no sidebar. 390: full width.

email uses `<wbr>` + `u-breakable`; one form per page, so ContactForm ids stay unique; the
success state's NextSteps h2 still fits the outline. Submit stays 44px on coarse (`c.submit`,
which is a PrimaryCTA).
